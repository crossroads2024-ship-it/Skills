# Phase 3: Video Factory

## Entry gate

Proceed only when the Phase 2 product is explicitly `APPROVED_FOR_CONTENT`. Require product identity, ASIN, evidence-backed facts, buyer problem, material caveats, affiliate URL or placeholder, and an asset plan with rights status. If anything is missing, return BLOCKED and list the missing items.

## Creative package

Produce one video around one buyer problem and one main benefit:

- 3 distinct hooks, normally 1–3 seconds each.
- 20–45 second vertical script, default 9:16.
- Selected hook, timed scenes, on-screen text and natural voiceover.
- Storyboard and practical shot list.
- SRT subtitles and plain voiceover text.
- Platform copy for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest.
- Clear affiliate disclosure and metadata.
- Human preflight checklist.

## Quality controls

Never fabricate product features, performance, savings, discounts, stock, ratings, testimonials or first-hand experience. Include a practical caveat where relevant. Avoid false urgency and keyword stuffing. Generated or illustrative visuals must not be presented as authentic product testing.

## Rights

Use original footage, user-provided assets or properly licensed material. Do not scrape or download Amazon listing media. Record source and rights status for every asset. Use no music unless its licence is clear; platform-cleared audio is preferable.

## Status

The engine creates drafts only. Human review is required before `fact_checked` and again before `approved_for_publish`. The skill never publishes.
