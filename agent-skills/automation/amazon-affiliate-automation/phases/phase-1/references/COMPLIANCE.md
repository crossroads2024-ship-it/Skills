# Compliance checklist

Before operating:

- Read the current Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and all applicable Programme Policies for the relevant marketplace.
- Confirm account eligibility and whether the account is approved for the intended social channels.
- Confirm which product data sources and API/feed permissions are available.
- Confirm the required Associate statement is clearly displayed on the relevant site/account.
- Put a clear affiliate disclosure close to each affiliate link or endorsement.
- Follow each social platform's branded-content and affiliate disclosure rules.
- Use only licensed or original creative assets.
- Do not imply Amazon sponsors or endorses the content.
- Do not fabricate personal use, product tests, reviews or product performance.
- Recheck current policies before changing the data pipeline or media workflow.

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice. Current programme terms control.
