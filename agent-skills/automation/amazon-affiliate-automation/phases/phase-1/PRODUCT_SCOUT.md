# Product Scout

## Goal

Find products worth further investigation, not merely products with high ratings. Prioritise clear consumer problems, demonstrable utility, content potential, evidence quality and manageable risk.

## Suggested score (0–100)

- Demand proxy, 0–25: review volume and other lawfully sourced demand signals.
- Quality proxy, 0–20: rating plus review count; ratings alone are weak evidence.
- Content potential, 0–20: visible/useful demonstration, clear problem solved and repeatable short-form angle.
- Economics, 0–20: price and commission assumptions supplied by the user or an authorised source. Do not assume a commission rate.
- Evidence confidence, 0–15: source quality, recency and completeness.

The included script uses conservative heuristics for supplied numeric fields. It cannot verify source quality or product suitability.

## Exclude or flag

- Safety-critical products or products making medical claims.
- Counterfeit, suspicious, recalled or poorly documented products.
- Products whose claims cannot be independently verified.
- Items dependent on copyrighted listing videos or images.
- Products with volatile pricing or availability where current verification is absent.
- Products where likely commission is too small to justify production effort.

## Required shortlist output

1. Product and source.
2. Why a buyer might want it.
3. Verified facts and evidence dates.
4. Proposed video angle.
5. Main limitation or risk.
6. Economics assumptions, clearly labelled.
7. Confidence: High / Medium / Low.
8. Decision: Research / Shortlist / Reject / Approved for Content.

A high score is a ranking signal, not a prediction of clicks, conversion, commissions or profit.
