# Compliance and rights controls

This project is operational guidance, not legal advice. Current Amazon Associates terms and each platform's policies control.

Before operating, verify account eligibility, permitted social channels, current rules for Amazon product data and Program Content, API/feed permissions, display/retention/refresh requirements, Associate statements, link-level affiliate disclosures and platform branded-content rules.

Never scrape Amazon, bypass access controls, use unauthorised feeds, invent product claims, fabricate personal testing or imply Amazon endorsement. Use original, user-owned or appropriately licensed creative assets. Record source and rights status. Keep credentials, tokens, private account data, local databases and private research exports out of Git.
